Opening Hand privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Opening Hand is a Pokémon TCG deck simulator and match journal for Android. This page says what data the app handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it. The short version: without an Account, everything stays on your phone. With an Account, your Decks, Matches and Tags are also stored online so they're safe and the same on every phone you use, and you can have Friends, chat with them and make Groups. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Who is responsible

Opening Hand is made by Marijn Goedhart, who is responsible for your data. Questions, requests and complaints: contact@openinghand.app.

Without an Account

Your Decks, Matches, Games, Tags and card notes are stored only in the app on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

The app does connect to the internet for two things, which don't include anything you logged:

  • Card data. Once a day it downloads the list of cards and archetypes from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).
  • Card images. When you search for cards it loads their pictures from images.pokemontcg.io and images.scrydex.com.

Like any website, these services see your phone's IP address when the app downloads from them.

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android also backs up the app's data to your Google account. That's between you and Google; see Google's privacy policy.

With an Account

Making an Account is optional. If you make one, we store:

  • Your email address, to log in, to send codes (confirming your Account, resetting your password) and to warn you about security changes, such as someone asking to remove two-factor login. Nobody else sees it, and we don't send newsletters or ads.
  • Your Username, shown in the app instead of your email.
  • Your password, if you set one, stored only in scrambled form (hashed); nobody can read it, including us.
  • Two-factor login, if you turn it on: the key your authenticator app shares with us, needed to check its codes, and your recovery codes, stored only hashed.
  • What you log in the app: Decks and their lists, Matches, Games, Tags, Card Tags and your answers about unknown cards, so they sync between your phones.
  • Friends: who your Friends are, the Friend Requests you sent and received, who you blocked, and what you chose to show your Friends. See Friends and Chats.
  • Chats: the Messages and Decks you send and receive, which Messages each of you has read, and Messages you reported.
  • Groups: the Groups you're in, with their name, description, badge and photo; who's in them and in what role; the Group Invites sent and received; your switches for each Group; Group Photos you reported; and, for Matches you log in a Group, which Group and which member you played. See Groups.
  • Meetups: the Meetups planned in your Groups, with their title, time, place, note and Spots; who made them; and your Answers (Going, Maybe or Can't go). See Groups.
  • When you last had the app open, to show your Friends that you're online, unless you turn that off.
  • Your phone's address for notifications (a Firebase Cloud Messaging token), so we can tell you about new Messages, unless you turn them off.
  • Technical data: when you logged in and from which IP address, kept by our provider to keep logins secure and to stop people guessing passwords.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your Google account's ID, email address, name and profile picture address. We use the ID and email to log you in; Supabase keeps the name and picture address with your login, but the app doesn't use or show them. Google learns that you signed in to Opening Hand; see Google's privacy policy. We don't get access to anything else in your Google account.

We use this only to run your Account, sync your data and let you talk with your Friends and Groups. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and safe, which includes looking at reported Messages and photos (article 6(1)(f)).

Friends and Chats

  • Finding you. Other players can find you only by typing your whole Username. They see nothing but your Username until you're Friends, and both of you have to agree to be Friends.
  • What Friends see. Only what you turn on under What friends see: your Deck lists, Deck stats and Recent Matches (all off at first), and whether you're online and have read their Messages (on at first; turned off, you don't see it of them either). Your notes, Events and Tags are never shown. You can hide a single Deck from Friends.
  • Messages. Chats are only between Friends. Your Messages, and the Decks you send, are stored on our server so they reach your Friend, and so your Chats are there on every phone. They are not end-to-end encrypted: they're encrypted on the way and stored securely, but the server can read them. To deliver them instantly, our provider's live delivery also keeps a copy for a few minutes. We don't look at Messages, except ones that are reported to us. A Sent Deck is a copy of the list, and of its Game Plan, stats and note if you add them; your Friend can save it as their own Deck.
  • Typing. While you're both in a Chat, your Friend sees when you're typing. This goes straight to their phone and isn't stored.
  • Reports. If someone reports one of your Messages, we keep a copy of it, with who sent and reported it and why, so we can look at it and act on it, for example by deleting an Account that harasses others. You're not told who reported it.
  • Blocking. When you block someone, they can't find you or send you Messages, their Requests don't reach you, and they aren't told.

Groups

  • Joining. Only a Friend who runs a Group (its Owner or an Admin) can invite you, and you're only in it once you accept. A Group has up to 50 members.
  • What members see. Every member sees the Group's name, description, photo and members: their Usernames and roles, also of members who aren't your Friends (they can send you a Friend Request from there). Leave the Group and you no longer see it, and its members no longer see you in it.
  • The group chat. It works like Chats: Messages and Sent Decks are stored on our server so every member gets them, also members who join later, and they're not end-to-end encrypted. Members see who's typing (not stored) and who has read a Message, following each member's online switch. The Owner and Admins can remove anyone's Message. When you leave, your Messages stay in the Group under your Username.
  • Group Photos. The Owner and Admins can give a Group a photo from their phone. The app crops it to a square and makes it small (512 × 512) before sending it, and leaves out the photo's hidden details, such as where it was taken. Only members can see it. Members can report it; a reported photo is kept, with who reported it and why, like a reported Message.
  • Group Matches and stats. When you log a Match in a Group, the Match says which Group, and which member you played if you pick one. Only if you turn on Show my stats in this Group (off at first, for each Group) do its members see your Record, win rate and most-played Deck there, and how your Matches against other members went, as you logged them. Decks you hid from Friends are never named; your notes, Events and Tags are never shown. Turn it off and they're gone from the Group's stats at once.
  • Meetups. Any member can plan a Meetup: a title, a time, and if they like a place, a note and a number of Spots. Every member sees it, who made it, and everyone's Answer (Going, Maybe or Can't go, and who's on the Waitlist), and gets a notification when one is planned, changed or cancelled, and a reminder 2 hours before if they said Going or Maybe. The place is text you type: the app doesn't use or collect your location. Open in Maps hands that text to your maps app, and Add to calendar hands the Meetup to your calendar app; what they do with it is up to them, and we don't get anything back. A Meetup's Results show how the Group Matches played during it went, following Show my stats in this Group like the Group's stats.

Where it's stored

Account data, Group Photos included, is stored with Supabase in their data centre in Frankfurt, Germany (EU), which stores and processes it on our behalf. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Emails are sent by Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), from their servers in Ireland (EU). Resend receives the email address and the email itself, only to deliver it, and keeps delivery logs for a short time. Because Resend is a US company, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. See Resend's privacy policy.

Notifications are delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging, a service of Google (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC, USA), on our behalf. To deliver a notification, Google receives your phone's notification address and the notification itself: your Friend's Username (and the Group's name, for a group chat) and the start of their Message, or, for a Meetup, its title and place. Google's Firebase SDK in the app also gives your phone an installation ID for this. The transfer to the USA is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. See Firebase's privacy information.

Emails to contact@openinghand.app are forwarded by Cloudflare to our inbox, without being stored by Cloudflare.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an Account. When you delete your Account, it's deleted 7 days later, together with everything in it, including your Friends and your Chats with them (for both of you), and your Messages and Answers in Groups. Meetups you planned stay in their Group, without your name. You leave your Groups; a Group you're the last member of is deleted, and one you own goes to another member. You can cancel during those 7 days. Our provider may keep technical logs for a short time after that. Your old Username stays reserved for 90 days so nobody else can pretend to be you.

  • Messages stay until the sender deletes them, or until one of you deletes their Account. A deleted Message is wiped from the server at once; only a reported copy stays (see Reports).
  • Groups stay until the Owner deletes them, or their last member leaves; then their chat, Messages, photo, Meetups and Invites are deleted. Group Invites stay until they're answered or cancelled. A Group Photo that's replaced or removed is deleted within a day.
  • Meetups stay with their Group, also after they took place, until the member who made it, the Owner or an Admin deletes one; then its Answers go too. When you leave a Group, your Answers to its upcoming Meetups are deleted; those to past Meetups stay under your Username, like your Messages.
  • Reports, of Messages (a reported Meetup with a copy of its title, place and note) and of Group Photos, are kept for 1 year, also when the Message, photo, Group or Account is deleted, so we can act on repeated harassment. Then they're deleted.
  • When you were last online: only the last time, and only while showing it is on. Turning it off deletes it.
  • Notifications waiting to be delivered are deleted after a day. Your phone's notification address is deleted when you log out on that phone, or when Firebase says it's no longer in use.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: Backup in the app saves everything you logged to a file. Your Chats are in the app; for a copy of them, email us.
  • Correct it: change anything in the app; change your Username or password under Account.
  • Delete it: delete your own Messages by long-pressing them; leave a Group, or turn off Show my stats in it; take back your Answer to a Meetup by tapping it again, or delete a Meetup you planned; turn off online status and notifications in the app; or Account → Delete Account in the app, or on the delete account page, or by emailing us from your Account's email address.
  • Object or ask questions: email contact@openinghand.app. We answer within a month.
  • Complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

If you're under 16, ask a parent or guardian before making an Account. The app works fully without one.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. Big changes are also announced in the app.
