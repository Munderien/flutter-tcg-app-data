What's in the app

Know your odds.

Paste a list and see how often you open with what you need: a Basic, your draw Supporter, your whole turn-one Game Plan, going first or second.

Log every Match.

Results in a few taps at the table, by Event and opponent, with win rates per Deck and per version of it.

Check your luck.

Luck Check compares what happened in your Games with what the odds said, so you know whether to change the list or just keep playing it.

Want it on two phones? Make an optional Account and your Decks and Matches sync. Without one, everything stays on your phone.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and there are no ads.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. Android comes first.

Do I need an Account?

No. Everything works without one. An Account only adds sync between phones and a backup in the cloud.

What happens to my data?

Without an Account, nothing leaves your phone. With one, read the privacy policy.

Which formats does it support?

Standard, with cards up to the newest set we've added.

Is this an official app?

No. Opening Hand is an unofficial fan-made app, not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.
