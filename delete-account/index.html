Delete your Opening Hand Account

You can also do this in the app: ⋮ → Account → Delete Account.

Deleting your Account removes it and everything in it from our servers: your email address, Username, Decks, Matches, Games and Tags. It happens 7 days after you ask, and you can cancel until then. What's on your phone stays there. To keep a copy, use Backup in the app first. Your old Username stays reserved for 90 days. See the privacy policy.
